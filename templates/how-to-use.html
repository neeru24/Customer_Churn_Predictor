Learn how to effectively use the Churn Predictor to retain your valuable customers using AI-powered insights.

📘 How to Use

Step 1: Open the Form

Click the "Get Started" button in the navbar to access the form.

Step 2: Fill in Customer Details

Provide necessary information such as tenure, charges, and contract type.

Step 3: Submit

Click on the submit button to send the data to the prediction engine.

Step 4: View the Result

The model will instantly show whether the customer is likely to churn.

🧾 What Each Field Means

📅 Tenure

How long (in months) the customer has been with your company.

💳 Monthly Charges

Monthly bill amount paid by the customer (e.g. ₹800).

🌐 Internet Service

Options include: DSL, Fiber optic, or No internet.

📄 Contract

Select from Month-to-month, One year, or Two year contracts.

📦 Services

Indicate extra services like online security, backup, streaming, etc.

🎯 Senior Citizen

Check Yes or No — helps personalize predictions.
