Step 1: Open the Form

Click the "Get Started" button in the navbar to access the prediction form.

Step 2: Fill in Details

Enter customer tenure, billing amounts, services, and contract types.

Step 3: Submit to Predict

Click on "🔍 Predict" to run the model and get a result instantly.

Step 4: View Result

See whether the customer is likely to churn, along with confidence %.

Step 5: Visualize with Charts

Interactive Bar, Doughnut, and Polar charts show churn stats visually.

Step 6: Export to PDF

Click "🧾 Export PDF" to save your prediction result and charts in one file.

🧾 What Each Field Means

📅 Tenure

How long (in months) the customer has been with your company.

💳 Monthly Charges

Monthly bill amount paid by the customer (e.g. ₹800).

🌐 Internet Service

Options include: DSL, Fiber optic, or No internet.

📄 Contract

Select from Month-to-month, One year, or Two year contracts.

🔐 Security & Support

Indicate extra services like online security, tech support, etc.

💰 Payment Method

Choose from: Electronic check, Credit card, Bank transfer, etc.
